# Code of conduct

## The short version

Be decent to people. Assume good faith. Critique code, not the person who wrote it.

## What we expect

- Welcome newcomers, and remember that everyone was one.
- Give feedback on the work: what is wrong, why it matters, and what would be
  better. "This is confusing" is a start; "this is confusing because X, and naming
  it Y would help" is a review.
- Take feedback on your own work as being about the work.
- Respect a decision once it is made and recorded, and reopen it with new
  information rather than repetition.

## What is not acceptable

Harassment, personal attacks, discriminatory language, publishing others' private
information, and sustained disruption of discussion. Anything that would make a
reasonable colleague not want to contribute here.

## Scope

This applies in issues, pull requests, reviews, commit messages, and anywhere else
people act on behalf of this project.

## Reporting

Raise a concern with the maintainers in [.github/CODEOWNERS](.github/CODEOWNERS).
Reports are handled discreetly. Maintainers may edit or remove contributions, and
may block accounts, where this document is not being followed.
